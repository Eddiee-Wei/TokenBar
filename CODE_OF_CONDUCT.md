# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

TokenBar contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, review ideas on technical merit, welcome constructive disagreement, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not permitted.
